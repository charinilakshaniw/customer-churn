# Customer Churn Retention Playbook

## High Risk Customers

Customers with a high predicted probability of churn should be prioritised
for immediate retention activity.

Recommended actions include:

- Personalised re-engagement communication
- Targeted retention offers
- Loyalty incentives
- Recommendations based on previous customer behaviour
- Review of recent customer engagement

High-value customers should receive priority when resources are limited.

## Medium Risk Customers

Medium-risk customers should be monitored and engaged proactively.

Recommended actions include:

- Personalised communications
- Product recommendations
- Loyalty messaging
- Monitoring transaction frequency

Aggressive discounts should generally be avoided unless there is evidence
that an incentive is likely to improve retention.

## Low Risk Customers

Low-risk customers should generally not receive aggressive retention
incentives.

Recommended actions include:

- Maintaining normal engagement
- Loyalty-building activities
- Personalised recommendations
- Cross-selling relevant products

## Customer Inactivity

Increasing days since the customer's last transaction can indicate declining
engagement.

Customers showing prolonged inactivity should be monitored for potential
churn.

## Retention Principle

Retention actions should be proportionate to both churn risk and customer
value.

The objective is to focus intervention on customers where retention activity
is most likely to provide business value.
